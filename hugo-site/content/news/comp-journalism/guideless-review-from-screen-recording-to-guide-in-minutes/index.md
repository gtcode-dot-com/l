## What is Guideless?

VIDEO

Guideless is an AI screen recorder that turns any online walkthrough into a narrated video guide and step-by-step documentation. Instead of manually recording a video, writing a script, recording a voiceover, and editing everything together, you just perform the task while Guideless captures it, then lets its AI handle the rest.

### How Guideless Actually Works

Guideless breaks the process down into three stages: Capture, Generate, and Publish.

First, you perform your task normally (clicking through a piece of software, filling out a form, whatever the workflow is) while the
[Guideless Chrome extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/guideless-ai-documentatio/ocgogifakflnbbkeejgepiancegepgem)
automatically records the steps in the background. The nice thing is, there’s no need to narrate out loud or plan what you’re going to say ahead of time.

From there, Guideless’s AI takes over. It writes step-by-step instructions based on what it captured, generates a natural voiceover to match, creates captions, and
[applies your branding](https://www.unite.ai/10-best-ai-graphic-design-tools/)
. You’re not stitching together a timeline. You’re reviewing and refining what the AI already built for you.

Once you’re happy with the result, publishing is the easy part. You can share a guide as a link, embed it, or export it as an MP4, PDF, or Markdown file. On the free plan, you’re limited to sharing via link and embed. Unlocking MP4 exports and removing the Guideless watermark requires upgrading to Pro.

### Guides That Update Themselves

One thing I appreciate about Guideless is that it doesn’t treat your guide as a locked video file. Instead, it separates the workflow into editable steps, so a guide behaves more like a living document than a video you’d have to fully re-record if one screen changes.

In other words, if a button moves or a step gets added, you can update just that piece instead of scrapping the whole recording and starting over. That’s a pretty big deal if you’re documenting software that changes often, which is most software.
