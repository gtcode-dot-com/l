Hey Siri, how much is the news worth to an AI assistant? The answer, according to
[a report](https://www.wsj.com/business/media/apple-in-talks-to-pay-publishers-to-improve-ai-powered-siri-0641f64b?st=RivHB3&amp;reflink=desktopwebshare_permalink)
by
[Alexandra Bruell](https://x.com/alexbruell)
in The Wall Street Journal, starts at $100 million.

Apple has approached publishers to use their news and up-to-date information in the revamped Siri AI, sources told the Journal. The company has reportedly discussed a “nine-figure budget” for the payments.

The next iteration of Apple’s voice-powered assistant was
[first promised in 2024](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2024-07-28/apple-intelligence-to-miss-initial-release-of-upcoming-ios-18-ipados-overhauls)
and is now expected to be released to the public later this year. (Beta reviewers seem to think
[it’s an](https://www.wired.com/story/siri-ai-hands-on-iphone/)
[improvement](https://www.theverge.com/tech/947432/siri-ai-apple-intelligence-ios-27-wwdc)
.)

Apple has proposed “a variable compensation plan” to news publishers, according to the Journal. The pay-as-you-go plan would see publishers paid only when Siri AI uses their content. It would be a break from

[other AI licensing agreements](https://www.niemanlab.org/2026/05/sam-altman-backs-micropayment-model-for-ai-agents-to-compensate-publishers/)

that have involved fixed fees and wide-ranging access to content. Apple currently makes payments to news companies for Apple News+

[using a similar model](https://www.niemanlab.org/2026/01/should-news-publishers-be-on-apple-news-a-u-k-report-finds-mixed-results/)

, paying publishers “proportionate to the share of clicks they generate within the platform.”
