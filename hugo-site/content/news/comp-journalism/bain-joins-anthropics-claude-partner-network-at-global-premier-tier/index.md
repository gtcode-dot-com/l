[Bain &amp; Company](https://www.bain.com/)
and Anthropic announced a global partnership on August 25, 2026, placing Bain in the Claude Partner Network at Global Premier, the program’s top tier. Under the agreement, Bain will work with Anthropic on client engagements spanning AI strategy, technology modernization, and AI-enabled operations, with
[the announcement](https://claude.com/blog/bain-company-joins-the-claude-partner-network-as-a-global-premier-partner)
positioning the work around moving enterprise clients from AI experiments to measurable deployments.

The partnership is anchored in a deployment that has already happened: Bain has rolled Claude out to its entire 19,000-person workforce, where it sits inside the firm’s suite of AI platforms and tools for both client work and internal operations. Consultants work with Claude.ai, Claude Cowork, Claude Code, Claude for Excel, and Claude for Microsoft 365 to build and test complex models, conduct research, and pressure-test inputs and assumptions.

The adoption numbers Bain disclosed are the kind partners usually keep vague. During the pilot phase, more than 7,000 employees were actively working with Claude within a few weeks of receiving access, and more than two-thirds of pilot participants took up Claude for Excel. The rollout paired that access with onboarding materials, trainings, webinars, and expert support, with user experience tracked through ongoing surveys — the enablement structure Bain now points to when advising clients on their own deployments.

“Our partnership with Anthropic brings together its frontier AI technology with Bain’s deep strategic and industry expertise to help clients turn the potential of AI into meaningful business results,” Philippe d’Arabian, Executive Vice President and Global Head of Partnerships at Bain &amp; Company, said in the announcement.

On the delivery side, clients get Claude models combined with Bain’s digital organization, which includes more than 1,500 AI, data, analytics, architecture, and engineering experts working alongside the firm’s industry and capability practices. Bain says that across multiple client engagements involving complex legacy codebases lacking architectural context, it has achieved productivity gains of 30% to 50%.

“More than 7,000 Bain employees were actively using Claude within weeks of the firm-wide deployment,” said Steve Corfield, global head of business development and partnerships at Anthropic. “The speed and breadth of that adoption signal what’s possible when the right technology is paired with the expertise to deploy it effectively, and that’s exactly what this partnership delivers for Bain’s employees and clients.”

## The Tier Structure Behind the Announcement

Global Premier is a defined threshold, not a marketing label. Anthropic launched the Claude Partner Network on March 12, 2026, backed by
[a $100 million commitment](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-partner-network)
to partner training, technical support, and joint market development, and then on June 3, 2026 added
[a three-tier Services Track](https://www.anthropic.com/news/services-track-partner-hub)
(Select, Preferred, and Global Premier) with published requirements at each level.

Reaching Global Premier requires a firm to hold at least 1,000 active certified individuals, at least 100 joint customers deployed in production across three or more regions, at least 15 public customer stories, and a joint business plan with named executive sponsors. Every partner’s standing against those requirements is visible in the Partner Hub’s public directory and refreshed daily, and Anthropic says it verifies standing quarterly. Promotions are processed twice a year, on January 1 and July 1, with an additional review on October 1, 2026 in this first year.

The tiering matters because it converts “partnership,” a word that in AI announcements can mean anything from a signed co-selling agreement to a logo on a slide, into a checklist an enterprise buyer can inspect. Anthropic has applied the same disclosed-numbers approach elsewhere on this beat: the
[Claude outage that hit Claude.ai, the API, Claude Code, and Cowork](https://www.unite.ai/claude-outage-hits-claude-ai-api-claude-code-and-cowork-as-errors-spread-across-models/)
on August 20, 2026 drew a public, service-by-service accounting rather than a generic incident note.

## A Crowded Bench of Consultancies

Bain joins a network where the largest professional-services firms have already staked out positions at scale.
[Accenture is training 30,000 professionals on Claude](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-accenture-partnership)
. Cognizant has rolled the model out to roughly 350,000 associates. KPMG is integrating Claude across a workforce of more than 276,000, and Infosys is building Claude-powered agents for specific industries.

The largest single deployment remains Deloitte’s expanded alliance,
[announced October 6, 2025](https://www.anthropic.com/news/deloitte-anthropic-partnership)
, which made Claude available to 470,000 people across Deloitte’s global network alongside a Claude Center of Excellence and a certification program for 15,000 professionals — at the time Anthropic’s largest enterprise AI deployment. Anthropic said in June 2026 that more than 40,000 firms had applied to join the network and more than 10,000 consultants had earned a Claude certification.

The competitive frame runs in both directions. OpenAI has been building the same kind of tiered services bench —
[KPMG reached the top of OpenAI’s partner network](https://www.unite.ai/kpmg-reaches-the-top-of-openais-partner-network/)
in July 2026, and
[EPAM joined at the Advanced tier](https://www.unite.ai/epam-joins-openais-partner-network-at-the-advanced-tier/)
later that month. Anthropic put $100 million behind the Claude Partner Network in March 2026 and OpenAI $150 million behind its own network in June 2026, both funding the consultancies that carry their models from pilot to production. Anthropic’s answer is the disclosed tier ladder; the next promotion window on October 1, 2026 will show which firms besides Bain can document their way to the top of it.
