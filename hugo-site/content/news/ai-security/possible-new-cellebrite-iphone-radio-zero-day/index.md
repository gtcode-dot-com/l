## Possible new Cellebrite iPhone Radio Zero-Day

Justin.sh is
[reporting](https://justin.sh/blog/2026/10/cellebrite-advanced-unlocks/)
on a confidential Cellebrite document that has been leaked to him:

&gt; Cellebrite calls it Advanced Unlocks (AU from here on), not to be confused with the “advanced unlocking services” they’ve marketed for years. A
&gt; [press release](https://web.archive.org/web/20260814034529/https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/cellebrite-appoints-shiven-ramji-chief-executive-officer-succeeding-thomas-e-hogan-302850249.html)
&gt; from earlier this year confirms AU is a new product for 2026.
&gt;
&gt; […]
&gt;
&gt; According to Cellebrite, AU:
&gt;
&gt; * is a “next-generation AFU vector that is more resilient to Apple’s OS changes”
&gt; * works with Stolen Device Protection enabled, which tightens USB restrictions
&gt; * is enabled by a dedicated appliance with a stated range (!) of about 8 meters.

AU = Advanced Unlocks, Cellebrite’s term. AFU = after first unlock.
