**Microsoft used its Ignite 2026 conference on Tuesday to announce the general availability of Perception, a product designed for coordinating teams of AI agents to handle security tasks.**

Unveiled in July as
[Project Perception](https://www.securityweek.com/microsoft-unveils-mai-cyber-1-flash-its-first-cybersecurity-ai-model/)
, the system has been in public preview inside Microsoft Defender since August.

Playbooks direct the agents, which are split into red, blue and green teams. Red agents play the attacker and probe for weaknesses; blue agents handle detection, alert triage, and investigations; and green agents fix problems.

The four blue team agents that are now generally available cover alert triage, attack investigation, detection authoring and threat intelligence. According to Microsoft, the Alert Triage Agent can handle roughly 80% of alerts in a typical Defender queue, based on an analysis of approximately 8 million alerts across its security products.

The Recon Agent, one of the red team agents, is also generally available, and a Web App Pen Test Agent has entered preview.

Two new green team playbooks work across Microsoft, AWS and Google Cloud. The agents behind them include Posture Prioritization, which is generally available, and Risk Reduction, which is in public preview.

The Risk Reduction Agent can recommend vulnerability shielding when a fix isn’t immediately available. This preview feature temporarily protects vulnerable assets until a permanent patch can be rolled out.

“Your team sets the guardrails and signs off on high-impact actions, and automated triggers launch playbooks when an incident meets the criteria you set,” Aleš Holeček, president and chief architect of Microsoft Security, wrote in a
[blog post](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/blog/2026/11/17/security-for-the-agentic-era-powered-by-microsoft-perception/)
.

Microsoft is also previewing integrations that link Perception to SIEM, SOAR and IT service management tools via MCP and APIs.

The company also addressed the risk posed by AI agents, which it views as a new kind of insider with broad access to data and tools.

In preview, Agent 365 and Entra Suite can detect 60 types of local AI agents, including Claude Code, OpenAI Codex and GitHub Copilot. Intune policies can keep supported agents on the device.
