## France Is Blaming Iran for the Fridge Cyberattack

[Interesting](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/09/world/europe/france-iran-refrigerators-cyberattack.html)

&gt; U.S. intelligence agencies have recently reached a “”ow confidence” assessment that Iran was responsible for the attacks in France, according to three U.S. officials who spoke on the condition of anonymity to discuss sensitive intelligence. &gt; The agencies’ judgment on the French incidents linked those attacks to Iran generally and not to a specific group such as the Revolutionary Guards, two of the officials said.
