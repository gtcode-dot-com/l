**A British Scattered Spider hacker will spend six years in a US federal prison for his part in a conspiracy to hack companies and take at least $11.6 million in cryptocurrency from individuals across the US.**

Tyler Robert Buchanan, 24, from Dundee, Scotland, was arrested in Spain in May 2024 and has been held by US authorities since April 2025. He
[pleaded guilty](https://www.securityweek.com/british-scattered-spider-hacker-pleads-guilty-in-the-us/)
in April 2026 to conspiracy to commit wire fraud and aggravated identity theft.

Between September 2021 and April 2023, the
[Scattered Spider](https://www.securityweek.com/topics/scattered-spider/)
group texted hundreds of phishing lures to staff at telecommunications, technology, interactive entertainment, cloud communications, and cryptocurrency companies.

Links in the texts pointed to phishing websites where recipients were tricked into entering credentials and personal information. Login details captured by the cybercriminals ended up in a Telegram channel managed by Buchanan and an accomplice.

With the stolen credentials, the hackers broke into employee accounts and company systems and took confidential information. That information helped them pinpoint and access cryptocurrency accounts and wallets owned by individuals.

To defeat two-factor authentication, they took over victims’ phone numbers through SIM swaps, moving the numbers to devices under their control.

The theft total has grown from the $8 million cited at the time of Buchanan’s plea. The court will hold a restitution hearing on December 11.

Buchanan is the final defendant in the
[five-person case](https://www.securityweek.com/us-charges-five-alleged-scattered-spider-members/)
to receive a sentence. Ahmed Hossam Eldin Elbadawy and Evans Onyeaka Osiebo, both of Texas, were each given 45 months in September and August, respectively.

[Noah Michael Urban](https://www.securityweek.com/scattered-spider-hacker-sentenced-to-prison/)
is serving 10 years in federal prison, while Joel Martin Evans received a two-year term.

Earlier this year, two other Scattered Spider members, Thalha Jubair and Owen Flowers, were
[sentenced](https://www.securityweek.com/two-scattered-spider-hackers-sentenced-to-jail-in-uk/)
to jail in the United Kingdom for a cyberattack targeting Transport for London (TfL).
