## Get expressive high-quality speech generation built for global scale

Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS delivers leading voice customization capabilities, securing the #1 overall spot on
[Hume AI’s](https://www.hume.ai/rw-voice-eq)
Voice Design Benchmark (71.4) and also leading in accent modeling (60.8).

Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS and Gemini 3.8 Flash-Lite TTS enable truly expressive performances without sacrificing reliability, also securing the #1 and #2 spots respectively on Hume AI’s Overall Quality Index. The model shows major improvements on a wide range of use cases such as long-form content and dual-speaker screenplay control compared to Gemini 3.1 Flash TTS.

In blind human preference evaluations on
[Voice Arena](https://voicearena.com/tts-leaderboard/us-english))
, Gemini 3.8 Flash and Flash-Lite TTS secure top positions amongst competitors in key global languages, including Japanese, Brazilian Portuguese, Vietnamese, Modern Standard Arabic (MSA), Mexican Spanish and Hindi. With support for over 100 languages, these models empower creators, developers, and enterprises to build high-quality, multilingual voice experiences worldwide.
