It’s a new way into the cloud.

[GeForce NOW](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/geforce-now/)

welcomes Firefox support to the cloud, opening up another way to jump into high-performance PC gaming straight from the browser, starting today. Whether on a
[school laptop](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/geforce-now-thursday-back-to-school-2026/)

or everyday PC, it’s now even easier to play supported PC games without downloading a dedicated app.

Plus, discover 12 new games streaming from the
[GeForce NOW library](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/geforce-now/games/)

this week, led by
*Gallipoli*

## **No Downloads. Just Firefox.**

[![GeForce NOW on Firefox](https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/gfn-firefox-browser-visual-kv-3840x2160-no-logo-1-1680x945.jpg)](https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/gfn-firefox-browser-visual-kv-3840x2160-no-logo-1-scaled.jpg)


This cloud is on fire with new ways to play on Firefox.

GeForce NOW is now
[supported on Firefox](https://blog.mozilla.org/en/firefox/firefox-nvidia-geforce-now-partnership/)

, expanding access across even more devices.

Members can outfox big downloads and jump into high-performance PC gaming directly from the browser — no waiting on lengthy game installs or updates. The experience delivers GeForce RTX-powered performance at up to 1440p and 120 frames per second for GeForce NOW Ultimate members.

Simply download the
[latest version of Firefox](https://www.firefox.com/en-US/landing/geforce-now/?utm_source=nvidia&amp;utm_medium=referral&amp;utm_campaign=firefox-geforce-now)

, head to
[play.geforcenow.com](https://play.geforcenow.com)

and start playing. The cloud handles the heavy lifting, so games are ready to play without taking up valuable storage space on the device. Firefox joins Chrome, Edge and Opera in supporting GeForce NOW on Windows browsers.
