**Research proposal · Version 0.1 · 15 November 2026.** CNS 9.0 proposes a falsification-guided discovery loop: turn conflicting accounts into measurable explanations, commit predictions, and seek observations that could refute them. No CNS 9.0 implementation or experimental results are reported.
