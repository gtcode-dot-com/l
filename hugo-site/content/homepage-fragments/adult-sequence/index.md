## I. Tax Office, Threats, and Criminal Prosecution (2015–2019)

1. <span id="account-1" class="home-account-anchor"></span>**[Young Kwak](https://tax.hawaii.gov/) — Coercion, false accusations, and false sworn testimony.** Verbally attacked and intimidated me inside a tiny, enclosed tax-office booth; demanded approximately seven times the actual tax owed; threatened consequences if I left; falsely accused me in the grand-jury proceedings; and falsely testified at trial that he merely instructed me which tax form to complete. His trial testimony concealed the coercive encounter. The prosecution also used a misleading diagram depicting the booth as substantially larger and more comfortable than it was.

   <span class="home-account-related">Related reporting: [The tax-office encounter](/disclosures/before-the-tax-office/#the-tax-office-encounter) · [Kwak’s testimony and the booth diagram](/hawaii-courts/the-hypothetical-gun/#the-witness-account-the-diagram-and-the-booth)</span>

2. <span id="account-2" class="home-account-anchor"></span>**[Unidentified New Jersey State Police investigator](https://www.njsp.gov/) — Threats, intimidation, and abuse of investigative authority.** During a November 2015 encounter, invoked my childhood acquaintances and organized-crime associations and threatened to give me a “colonoscopy,” communicating the threat of an invasive, humiliating investigation. James Yuen stood beside him during the encounter.

   <span class="home-account-related">Related reporting: [The November 2015 investigator encounter](/disclosures/before-the-tax-office/#november-2015-yuen-and-the-colonoscopy-threat)</span>

3. <span id="account-3" class="home-account-anchor"></span>**[Gene (Eugene) Hartmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Army_Security_Agency) — Death threats, coercion, and witness intimidation.** While I was under indictment and investigating for my defense, instructed me to “Stay away from [name withheld],” knowing about my prosecution and defense investigation. Participated in the death threat delivered with Rita Hartmann, pressured me to leave Hawaiʻi, and subsequently reframed the threatening exchange as a “murder mystery.”

   <span class="home-account-related">Related reporting: [The Hartmann threat](/hawaii-courts/the-threat-report/#a-death-threat-to-stop-my-defense-investigation) · [The threat and counsel report](/disclosures/before-the-tax-office/#the-hartmann-threat-and-the-counsel-report)</span>

4. <span id="account-4" class="home-account-anchor"></span>**Rita Hartmann — Death threats and coercion.** Participated in the same encounter, supplying the lethal consequence of Gene's instruction: “Or you will get whacked.” Also participated in the pressure for me to leave. The threats were directed at conduct connected to my ongoing defense investigation and intended disclosures.

   <span class="home-account-related">Related reporting: [The Hartmann threat](/hawaii-courts/the-threat-report/#a-death-threat-to-stop-my-defense-investigation)</span>

5. <span id="account-5" class="home-account-anchor"></span>**“Kevin” — Intimidation, retaliation, and threats against my career.** Approached me outside Kahala Whole Foods shortly before my July 2017 trial and threatened to destroy my career if I continued discussing the Hartmann threat and associated conduct. Invoked a public figure, that person's spouse, and a prominent technology investor to reinforce the warning.

   <span class="home-account-related">Related reporting: [The pretrial career warning](/disclosures/before-the-tax-office/#may-or-june-2017-the-career-warning)</span>

6. <span id="account-6" class="home-account-anchor"></span>**[Vincent Kanemoto](https://ag.hawaii.gov/) — Prosecutorial misconduct, prejudicial jury manipulation, and dishonesty.** During closing argument in July 2017, acknowledged that no gun was involved, formed a two-handed mock pistol, pointed it toward all twelve jurors, asked them to imagine a gun anyway, and urged conviction. The judge immediately intervened and summoned counsel into chambers. The prosecution's trial presentation omitted Kwak's earlier charging accusation and the recordings documenting my distress, while presenting his false routine-form account and a misleading booth diagram. Kanemoto worked directly with James Yuen preparing the case. When I confronted Kanemoto at Glazer's in Haleʻiwa during summer 2019, he denied making the mock-pistol gesture.

   <span class="home-account-related">Related reporting: [The mock-pistol closing argument](/hawaii-courts/the-hypothetical-gun/#the-imaginary-gun) · [The later denial](/hawaii-courts/the-hypothetical-gun/#a-later-denial)</span>

7. <span id="account-7" class="home-account-anchor"></span>**[Audrey L.E. Stanley](https://lawyers.findlaw.com/hawaii/honolulu/5063522_1/) — Neglect of defense obligations, dismissal of a death threat, and deficient client communication.** As my assigned public defender, interrupted and dismissed my report of the Hartmann death threat, characterized it as a “conditional death threat” and “no big deal,” and failed to explain reporting, investigative, or protective measures. Separately laughed while reviewing the grand-jury account of my distress. Also relayed a prosecution-originated proposal that the case might be resolved if I left Hawaiʻi, without providing clear terms or explaining its implications. Stanley subsequently became a per diem Family Court judge.

   <span class="home-account-related">Related reporting: [The report to Stanley](/hawaii-courts/the-threat-report/#what-stanley-heard-from-me) · [The departure proposal](/hawaii-courts/the-threat-report/#the-leave-hawaiʻi-communication)</span>

8. <span id="account-8" class="home-account-anchor"></span>**Unnamed Retained Trial Counsel — Conflict of interest involving the high priest, abandonment of a reported death threat, ineffective assistance, deficient representation, and coercive plea pressure.** Had a conflict-of-interest issue involving the high priest that raised serious questions about his loyalty and independent judgment. I personally informed him that Gene and Rita Hartmann had threatened to kill me while I was under indictment and conducting my defense investigation. He dismissed the murder threat, failed to treat the witness-intimidation implications seriously, failed to explain investigative or protective options, and advised me against including the threat in my trial testimony. I followed his advice, keeping an actual death threat and its connection to my defense investigation out of the jury's hearing. His handling of that report must be examined alongside the high-priest conflict, not treated as an isolated tactical decision.

   Charged $6,000 for plea representation and $8,000 for trial, with no specified compensation for retrial. Told me the judge wanted a “tight trial,” said he was counting on me looking guilty, and opened with words suggesting “Maybe he did it.” Conducted cursory cross-examination of Young Kwak, dismissed my immediate report of Kwak's false sworn testimony, failed to obtain the requested photograph of the enclosed tax-office booth, and failed to adequately challenge the prosecution's misleading diagram.

   Following Vincent Kanemoto's mock-pistol closing argument and the judge's immediate chambers conference, failed to adequately explain mistrial remedies, preservation of prosecutorial misconduct, or available legal options. During jury deliberations, introduced and repeatedly pressured me to accept a petty-misdemeanor plea despite my explicit refusals. Scolded me while I prayed, saying he did not believe in “that religion stuff,” and continued pressing the plea. Never explained that an unconditional plea would ordinarily foreclose direct appellate review of the prosecutor's earlier misconduct, nor advised me about disciplinary reporting options. After the hung jury, initially told me another trial was expected, then expressed pleasure when Kanemoto decided not to retry, reporting that the prosecutor was “spooked.”

   **Conflict-of-interest concerns include the high-priest connection, compromised loyalty in handling my murder-threat report, financial incentives to avoid retrial, and professional interests in ending the prosecution without further scrutiny of the misconduct.**

   <span class="home-account-related">Related reporting: [Counsel’s advice about testimony](/hawaii-courts/the-defense-table-went-quiet/#my-testimony-and-state-of-mind) · [Plea pressure and appellate consequences](/hawaii-courts/the-defense-table-went-quiet/#the-appellate-consequence-he-did-not-explain)</span>

9. <span id="account-9" class="home-account-anchor"></span>**James Yuen — Investigative involvement, intimidation-related conduct, and prosecution information-flow concerns.** Stood beside the purported New Jersey investigator during the threatening November 2015 encounter. ## III. Judicial Misconduct and Attorney Conduct (2022)


13. <span id="account-13" class="home-account-anchor"></span>**[Judge Wilson M.N. Loo](https://disclosures.civilbeat.org/disclosures/wilson-loo-2-2/) — Judicial interference, manipulation of sworn testimony, suppression of objections, unequal treatment, and denial of a fair hearing.** During the December 2, 2022 hearing, interrupted and curtailed my presentation of stalking, assault, harassment, and vehicle-attack evidence, saying in substance that I could go on all day. During a material question about the defendant's conduct, turned toward the sworn witness and signaled “no” with his head and facial expression immediately before the witness denied the conduct. When I immediately attempted to preserve the gesture on the record — “Let the record show that the judge just—” — shouted, “Nah ah ah enough out of you!!” The hearing was recorded by audio only, leaving his visual action outside the recording. Permitted the opposing party's false claim during final remarks without allowing my rebuttal. Denied my requested injunction and granted Petricevic's sealing request, restricting outside examination of the proceedings. His conduct raises judicial impartiality, witness-interference, due-process, and civil-rights violations.

    <span class="home-account-related">Related reporting: [The reported signal and interrupted record statement](/hawaii-courts/the-nod-visual-allegation/#the-courtroom-sequence) · [The interrupted presentation of evidence](/hawaii-courts/wilson-loo-judicial-signaling/#the-case-loo-cut-off)</span>

14. <span id="account-14" class="home-account-anchor"></span>**[Bosko Petricevic](https://boskolaw.com/meet-your-attorney/) — Suppression-related conduct, professional misconduct, and judicial-misconduct reporting concerns.** Represented the defendant at the December 2, 2022 hearing. Before the hearing, offered and urged me to accept a cross-injunction resolution that would have avoided live testimony. During the disputed sworn-testimony sequence, was seated facing the bench, and I personally observed him looking toward Judge Loo as the judge gave the visual signal to his client. Petricevic requested sealing of the court record, which Loo granted, restricting public access to the testimony, signaling sequence, interruption, and hearing outcome. His conduct raises professional-responsibility questions concerning the handling of judicial interference and the mandatory reporting duty under **Hawaiʻi Rule of Professional Conduct 8.3(b)**. I have received no account from him identifying what he saw, understood, or reported.

## Case outcomes {#case-outcomes}

The 2017 prosecution ended in a hung jury, no retrial, and subsequent expungement. My later criminal complaints produced no prosecution of the reported defendant. The December 2, 2022 hearing record was sealed. The Commission on Judicial Conduct closed its earlier complaint and declined renewed review. I subsequently referred the matter to federal authorities, including the DOJ Public Integrity Section, which acknowledged receipt of my July 12, 2025 submission.
