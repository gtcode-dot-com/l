Gene Hartmann told me, “Stay away from [name withheld].” Rita Hartmann added, “Or you will get whacked.” When I reported the threat to my assigned lawyer, Audrey Stanley, she called it a “conditional death threat” and dismissed it, in substance, as “no big deal.” Years later, Officer Brandt prevented another officer from receiving my report of the same threat.

I reported an attempt to run me down on PaaLaa Road: a driver accelerated from behind, crossed the double yellow line toward me, narrowly missed me, and raced away. HPD marked the entry **“MISC PUB”** without identifying the driver or recording the attack in an incident narrative. An HPD detective later asked whether I would pay taxes on approximately $200 the man I reported had never paid me. I sought protection in court. The opposing lawyer portrayed my cycling on a public path as stalking, although his client approached me from behind in a vehicle.

At the December 2, 2022 hearing, I asked the defendant, who was under oath, whether he had furnished the substance during a Stonefish Grill transfer I personally witnessed. I was looking at Judge Wilson M.N. Loo as I asked. Loo turned toward the defendant, looked directly at him, and made a broad, unmistakable side-to-side “no” head movement. Both the defendant and his lawyer were looking at Loo. The defendant then answered “no.” **That denial was false, and I accuse him of knowingly committing perjury under [HRS § 710-1060](https://data.capitol.hawaii.gov/hrscurrent/Vol14_Ch0701-0853/HRS0710/HRS_0710-1060.htm).** I immediately began, “Let the record show that the judge just—,” but Loo shouted me down before I could describe the movement for the audio-only record. He denied my requested injunction, the defendant obtained an injunction, and Loo granted opposing counsel Bosko Petricevic’s request to seal the proceedings. **I accuse Loo of willfully depriving me of due process under color of law, in violation of [18 U.S.C. § 242](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/t18/s242/).** My § 242 accusation stands separately from the further question: **did Loo suborn the perjury by deliberately inducing or assisting the witness’s false answer?**

I name the people involved and describe what I saw and heard. The dates, report numbers, and outcomes are here so that each person and institution can answer for the conduct attributed to them.

## I. Death threats and pressure to stay silent

1. <span id="account-3" class="home-account-anchor"></span>**[Gene (Eugene) Hartmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Army_Security_Agency) — “Stay away,” then a “murder mystery.”** I knew Gene and Rita Hartmann through the Haleʻiwa duplex where I rented the back unit and they occupied the front.    Before that encounter, a man in Aiea had also told me to stay away from the same person. I can identify him by sight. I had been asking questions in a local technical community I helped establish. The repeated instruction and Gene and Rita’s delivery led me to believe their threat had been prepared in advance. I want each speaker asked who supplied information about my inquiries, whether anyone suggested or arranged the warning, what each discussed before and afterward—including through intermediaries—and what calls, messages, or witnesses can establish those contacts.

    - **June 2022, Breakers / North Shore Marketplace:** Another aggressive vehicle approach and intimidation.

HPD marked the entry **“MISC PUB.”** It contains no driver identification or narrative of the vehicle attack I reported, and Shatoo took no formal statement from me. After my efforts to obtain effective police intervention failed, I posted about the attack in local Facebook groups to alert the community and seek help.     - **September 16–22, 2022:** I submitted my protective-order petition at Wahiawa Police Station on September 16. The harassment continued. **HPD reference 22-365099** concerns the September 20 Breakers encounter. ## IV. Loo's “no” signal during perjury, the silenced record, sealing, and deprivation of rights under color of law (18 U.S.C. § 242)

14. <span id="account-13" class="home-account-anchor"></span>**[Judge Wilson M.N. Loo](https://disclosures.civilbeat.org/disclosures/wilson-loo-2-2/) — Signaling “no” to a sworn witness, silencing my record statement, sealing, and my 18 U.S.C. § 242 accusation.** At the December 2, 2022 hearing in Honolulu, I represented myself and sought protection from the assault, stalking, harassment, and vehicle attacks. I arrived with a stack of documents. When I tried to explain the incidents, Loo cut me off and said, in substance, that I could go on all day. He did not ask me for a short account or let me finish presenting the conduct for which I sought protection.

    Before the question concerning the Stonefish Grill transfer, I had submitted a text-message exhibit relevant to the transfer being addressed in court. It supplied context for my question about the witness’s conduct. The exhibit remains in the sealed court file.

    I asked the defendant, who was testifying under oath, whether he had furnished the substance at Stonefish Grill. I was looking at Loo as I asked. He turned his head toward the defendant, looked directly at him, and made a broad, unmistakable side-to-side “no” head movement, scrunching his nose and pursing his lips. I saw both the defendant and his lawyer, Bosko Petricevic, looking toward Loo at that moment. The defendant then answered “no.” That was his sworn denial of the transfer I witnessed; I accuse him of knowingly giving false testimony.

    I immediately began: “Let the record show that the judge just—” Loo cut me off, shouting, “Nah ah ah enough out of you!!”

    The hearing was recorded by audio only. Loo’s gesture was therefore outside the recording, and he stopped my immediate attempt to describe it aloud.

    Loo later allowed the opposing party to address the court during final remarks while stopping me from responding. He denied my requested injunction; the opposing party obtained an injunction, and Loo granted Petricevic's request to seal the court record. Sealing restricted outside access to the audio recording of Loo's interruption and the file containing the text-message exhibit. **I accuse Loo of willfully depriving me of a fair hearing and the opportunity to present, challenge, and preserve material evidence under color of law, in violation of [18 U.S.C. § 242](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/t18/s242/).** The acts I challenge are his signal to a witness immediately before false sworn testimony, his interruption of my attempt to preserve the gesture in an audio-only record, his restrictions on my presentation and response, and his sealing order. **Beyond that § 242 accusation, did Loo suborn the perjury — intentionally inducing or assisting the witness to give the false answer?** That is the question raised by [HRS § 710-1072 (witness tampering)](https://data.capitol.hawaii.gov/hrscurrent/Vol14_Ch0701-0853/HRS0710/HRS_0710-1072.htm) and [§ 702-222 (complicity)](https://data.capitol.hawaii.gov/hrscurrent/Vol14_Ch0701-0853/HRS0702/HRS_0702-0222.htm). Loo had himself served on the Commission on Judicial Conduct. What purpose did his gesture serve, why did he prevent my immediate record statement, and why seal the resulting record?

    [Rutgers Law’s August 2024 alumni account](https://law.rutgers.edu/alumni-updates-1) identifies Loo as a 1980 graduate and a former organized-crime prosecutor for Honolulu. His professional experience makes his handling of the evidence and my attempt to preserve the signal a question he should answer directly.
