TRACE is an unvalidated, unimplemented research proposal. The authoritative text is latex/manuscript.md; its generated TeX and Hugo editions share that source. Source formatting and document production do not establish scientific validity.

## Scientific commitments

- H2 is the primary question: added predictive value of supplied conflict localization against a contrastive discovery adaptation with identical evidence and cost.
- H1 is supporting reliability measurement. H3 is a conditional later investigation study.
- Development (12–20 cases) and exploratory pilot (30–60) are proposed feasibility phases, not powered confirmation.
- Static and adaptive prediction are separate; missing measurements remain unknown. A variable conjecture is not an observation.
- Source lineage does not prove independence or truth. Unit/estimand alignment and copying detection earn no novel-discovery credit.
- A/B/C/D comparisons distinguish refinement, contrastive discovery, conflict-directed generation and explicit lineage handling. D–C tests the conditional lineage-accounting package, including weights, eligibility and warrant rules; no isolated weighting effect or independent lineage main effect is claimed.
- The C–B mechanism comparison uses a shared candidate selector without system identity, conflict labels or generation order. A source-summary preprocessing control addresses additional processing; a separate transfer comparison augments an existing method with generated localization while retaining native search/scoring, equal evidence and a source-summary control.
- The explanatory relationship and parameters are locked before prediction contexts are revealed. The primary prediction endpoint uses point predictions and evaluator-fixed tolerances, with diagnostic settings required; broad intervals do not earn prediction credit. All predefined eligible cases remain in the denominator.
- Common-input and end-to-end results must be separate. Oracle-corrected conflict records are diagnostic only. Generated localization consumes the common budget.
- The numerical compiler is an implementation option. Affordable exhaustive classical search is unrestricted; the 64-condition prefix is a separate diagnostic.
- The action-separation heuristic has no inherited POPPER sequential guarantee. Implication and conditional test-validity assumptions must be met before such guarantees are invoked.
- No large synthetic grid or prospective execution count is committed. Later samples, quality margins, effects, power and multiplicity require independent justification.
- The caching formula and all diagram values are hypothetical, not measured outcomes.
