# TRACE v0.1 — source and claim audit

**Scope:** provenance and evidential boundaries of an unvalidated research proposal. Document production checks do not validate a scientific theory.

## Research inputs

The five-document TRACE packet supplied the research review, theory/specification, experimental design, execution planning and an annotated inventory of 45 sources. The public manuscript and companions consolidate that material; they are not a record of executed experiments. The original `paper.tex` and `aal.tex` are David Lopez-Paz's Against All Likelihood inputs and are not redistributed as TRACE manuscript source.

Earlier CNS proposals already contain uncertainty, missing-access, proof and unresolved-conflict safeguards. TRACE revises empirical acceptance and proposes a testable composition; it does not claim prior versions certified metaphysical truth. Geometric/tensor diagnostics remain optional comparisons rather than mandatory scientific foundations.

## Source review depth

The current registry contains 55 annotated records: 45 inherited from the input packet and ten further related-work entries (R31–R40). The actual depth is disclosed per record and ranges from metadata/abstract inspection to selected methods and supplied full text. It is not a claim that every paper, proof or codebase was independently audited.

For the closest added comparisons, HypoGeniC's primary v3 introduction/method sections and the official NLP4Science 2024 record establish iterative hypothesis-bank updating and venue metadata (R37). Qiu et al.'s primary v4 abstract and experimental-method framing establish rule proposal/selection/refinement and the induction/application distinction (R38). The institutional ICLR 2024 Causal Modelling Agents record and opening description establish hybrid metadata/data-driven causal modeling (R39); no implementation reproduction or general active-intervention guarantee is inferred. The unrelated arXiv:2402.09626 is not cited as this work. The source URLs and limitations are in the registry.

Prior-art support concerns components and related methods, not proof that the TRACE procedure is novel or effective. The paper makes no exhaustive priority claim. Author-released methods adapted to the proposed benchmark must disclose their implementation choices before evaluation.

## Claims and operational limits

- H1, H2 and H3, including +5/+10 percentage-point targets, are untested hypotheses.
- Integrity checks, rival-indexed response commitments and evaluator-only relations are distinct. Semantic validation can remain unknown; runtime has no gold mechanism oracle.
- The proposed pairwise set-separation policy is a heuristic with explicit eligibility/cost/tolerance rules. It is not exact Bayesian information gain or logical refutation of noisy models.
- Warrant follows evidence/assumption and defeat dependencies, not a scalar weakest-link order. All-inadequate candidate sets cannot produce an accepted least-bad explanation.
- Narrative disagreement as a useful search prior is a mechanism hypothesis. Required shared-extraction and refinement comparisons could explain away its apparent benefit.
- The caching formula is invented, with dimensionless variables and explicit denominators. External scheduled contention is distinct from treatment-induced backend utilization. Freshness failures are scored, not discarded.
- F8 identifiability is target/view-specific. Causal equivalence can coexist with identifiable observational predictions; infeasible family/class allocations must change before registration.
- The ten-system grid, 2,400 roots, 1,200 discovery cases, 1,800 actionable cases and all derived quantities are conditional planning targets. No benchmark or model evaluation has been executed.
- Absolute false-promotion/resolution limits are retained. Sampling-valid interval methods, power, component gates, tolerances, generator feasibility and prospective clustering remain explicit freeze decisions.
- Scenario inference is conditional on the service; 240 workload seeds are not 240 independent mechanism replications.

## Publication and licensing

Full web and PDF editions share one manuscript and bibliography. Citation formatting and figure serialization differ by medium. The accessible guide and evaluation page are complementary documents, not empirical replications. No DOI, peer review, accepted result or live deployment is created by packaging these files. Third-party sources are linked with attribution; their text, code, data and licenses are not silently reassigned.

## Method scope

TRACE specifies alignment, lineage accounting, rival generation and investigation. Its cross-case schema distinguishes source statements, extraction inferences, candidate conjectures and evaluator outcomes. The generator may introduce measurable variables but cannot supply missing observations by inference. The numerical compiler is one prediction mechanism; its search-order experiment is separate from generation and end-to-end evaluation. The 64-condition diagnostic is not a baseline handicap. Engineering limits and fit penalties require development-stage justification before confirmation.

R40 supplies close prior art for LLM-based rule inference with experimental design. Its official NeurIPS abstract and opening description support that comparison; no claim of code reproduction or exhaustive literature coverage is made. A positive H2 comparison would not by itself establish which TRACE stage caused an improvement.
